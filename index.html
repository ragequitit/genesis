The loop

Hold a node. Spend what it earned. Go further out.

Mine the node

Machines pull data around the clock. Upgrade them and the numbers keep climbing while you're away.

Learn two trades

Pick one gathering profession and one crafting profession. That pairing is yours — the rest you trade for.

Trade with players

The Auction House is a real market. List what you make, buy what you can't. Listing costs almost nothing; the tax is taken when it sells.

Kit yourself out

Fourteen slots, six rarities, affixes that actually change the run. Salvage what you outgrow.

Run expeditions

Send yourself out on timed jobs across sixteen regions. Fuel gets you there; loot comes back.

Raise a companion

Companions level with you and lend a bonus that shapes how you play — more loot, faster runs, richer sales.
